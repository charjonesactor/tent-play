Welcome to the tent

Women gather monthly in the tent to support, commiserate, and celebrate each other as they move through the ridiculous, painful, heartbreaking, and joyful experience of female biology over the course of their reproductive years.

Tent Play follows five women from puberty through menopause, and invites the audience to be part of the journey through games and facilitated engagement.

In this tent, we are all women and we are all welcome.

You're in the play

The show is staged in the round, with the audience as part of the design. Clown-inspired, real-time theatrical games mean every performance is different, depending on who is in the tent that night.

The Game of Womanhood

The audience is asked “What else is expected of a woman?” and the company acts out their answers.

The Egg of Guilt

Help deliver the egg as a character labors and births her emotional baggage.

Chanting Hemorrhoids

Join the chorus. Yes, really.

After every performance, stay for drinks in the lobby. We'll also host post-show talkbacks, a panel with medical professionals on perimenopause, and talking circles for building community near you.

About Julia Proctor

Founding Director of Clown Gym, NYU Tisch Drama faculty member, and co-founding producer of PhysFestNYC. Julia is an accomplished theatrical clown, director, performer, educator, and community builder. Tent Play is her first scripted play, bringing together more than a decade of experience creating ensemble-driven, audience-centered theatre.
